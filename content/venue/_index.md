        ## Getting to Grenoble

        **By Air**
        - **Lyon Saint-Exupéry Airport (LYS)** — approximately 1 hour by shuttle or train. It is the most convenient international airport.
        - **Geneva International Airport (GVA)** — approximately 2 hours by train.
        - **Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG)** — approximately 3 hours by TGV + regional train.

        **By Train**
        Grenoble has direct TGV connections from Paris (approx. 3 hours), Lyon (approx. 1h15), and Marseille. 

        **By Car**
        Grenoble is accessible via several motorways from Lyon and Marseille/Aix-en-Provence.

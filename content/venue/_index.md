        ## About Grenoble

        Grenoble is a vibrant city at the foot of the French Alps, situated at the confluence of the Isère and Drac rivers. Known as the *Capital of the Alps*, it combines a rich scientific and academic tradition with stunning mountain scenery.

        Grenoble is home to several major research institutions, including:
        - [Université Grenoble Alpes (UGA)](https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/)
        - [CNRS](https://www.cnrs.fr/) laboratories
        - [Inria Grenoble — Rhône-Alpes](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-grenoble-rhone-alpes)
        - The European Synchrotron Radiation Facility (ESRF) and Institut Laue-Langevin (ILL)

        The city has a strong tradition in mathematics, computer science, and image analysis, making it an ideal host for DGMM 2027.

        ## Getting to Grenoble

        **By Air**
        - **Lyon Saint-Exupéry Airport (LYS)** — approximately 1 hour by shuttle or train. It is the most convenient international airport, with connections throughout Europe and beyond.
        - **Geneva International Airport (GVA)** — approximately 2 hours by coach or train.
        - **Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG)** — approximately 3 hours by TGV + regional train.

        **By Train**
        Grenoble has direct TGV connections from Paris (approx. 3 hours), Lyon (approx. 1h15), and Marseille. The train station is centrally located.

        **By Car**
        Grenoble is accessible via the A48 motorway from Lyon and the A51 from Marseille/Aix-en-Provence.

        ## Local Information

        Grenoble offers a wealth of things to see and do:
        - The **Bastille** fortress and cable car with panoramic views of the Alps
        - The **Musée de Grenoble**, one of France's leading fine arts museums
        - World-class skiing and hiking in the surrounding Belledonne, Vercors, and Chartreuse massifs
        - A lively city centre with restaurants, cafés, and cultural venues
